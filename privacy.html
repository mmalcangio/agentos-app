AgentOS privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

AgentOS is a personal application used only by its owner, Michael Malcangio. It is not available to anyone else.

What data it accesses

With the owner's consent through Google sign-in, AgentOS requests read-only access to the owner's own YouTube account data (scope youtube.readonly), such as playlists and liked videos.

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to let the owner search and organise his own information. It is stored on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party, and it is not used for advertising.

AgentOS's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored data can be deleted by the owner from his own computer.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/mmalcangio/agentos-app.
